# Research summary (2026-11-04)

Sources were gathered with web search. Store pages and some trackers could not be opened directly from the build environment, so several figures are third-party estimates (AppMagic, Sensor Tower) and are marked as such. Verify before relying on them for decisions.

Market notes: hybridcasual IAP grew 37% year over year in 2024 ([Sensor Tower](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sensor_tower__state_of_mobile_gaming_2025__en.pdf)). Median lifetime revenue for new 2026 RPGs that passed $1M was $2M ([AppMagic via mgoddess](https://www.mgoddess.com/news/new-mobile-rpgs-hit-1m-twice-as-fast-as-puzzle-but-puzzle-s-playing-the-/?lang=en)). Retention: top 10% D1 ≈ 40%, top 25% D7 ≈ 6–7% ([GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks)); medians ≈ D1 22%, D7 ~4%, D30 <1% (secondary summary). No reliable public payer-conversion benchmark was found.

## Naming
"Relic Hunters" is an existing franchise by Rogue Snail: Zero (2015), Rebels on Netflix mobile ([Netflix](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/relic-hunters-first-ever-mobile-game-now-exclusively-on-netflix)) and Legend with Gearbox (2025). "Relic + word" titles are crowded (Relic Quest, Relic Rumble, Relic Rush, Relic Looter, Relic Wilds, Relic Arena). For "Legend of Kitsune", no exact game was found in a quick search, but [Legend of Hero Fox: Idle RPG](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6502819449) is a fox-themed idle RPG. A formal trademark search is required.

## Platforms and policy
- Godot 4.7 stable (June 2026), 4.7.1 (July) ([Godot](https://godotengine.org/article/maintenance-release-godot-4-7-1/)). iOS C# still experimental ([forum](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/is-the-ios-support-good-enough-when-using-the-mono-version-for-a-full-release/128950)). Reported iOS launch issue with 4.6.2 and Xcode 26.4 ([forum](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/godot-4-6-2-xcode-26-4-physical-ios-device-launch-fails/138380)).
- IAP: official GodotGooglePlayBilling ([docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/platform/android/android_in_app_purchases.html)); third-party [godot-iap](https://hyochan.github.io/godot-iap) (StoreKit 2 + Billing 8+); [Godotx RevenueCat](https://store.godotengine.org/asset/paulo/godotx-revenuecat/).
- Unity: Personal free under $200K revenue and funding; Pro $2,310 per seat per year from Jan 2026 ([Unity](https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates)).
- Apple: Xcode 26 / iOS 26 SDK required for uploads from 2026-04-28 ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/)); 15% Small Business Program; new 13+/16+/18+ age ratings ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ks775ehf)); loot-box odds disclosure (guideline 3.1.1); US storefront allows external purchase links since May 2025 ([Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/05/02/updated-app-review-guidelines-us)).
- Google: new personal accounts need 12 testers for 14 days ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)); target API 36 ([Android](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk)); new fee structure from 2026-09-30 in the US/UK/EEA, with a service fee plus a 5% billing fee ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/google-play-expands-billing-options-and-lowers-fees-for-developers/)). Verify exact rates in Play Console.
- Regulation: FTC $20M settlement with HoYoverse over confusing currencies and odds ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/hoyoverse-agrees-to-20-million-settlement-with-ftc-over-loot-boxes)); Brazil bans loot boxes for minors from 2026-03-17 ([Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/childrens-privacy/brazil-adopts-law-protecting-minors-online/)); EU CPC principles on virtual currencies ([Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2025/03/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper)); EU Digital Fairness Act proposal expected late 2026 ([Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/en/news-events/newsletter-articles/detail/digital-fairness-act-implementation-obligations-companies-need-to-prepare-for.html)).
- AI art: the US Copyright Office says purely AI-generated material is not protected ([Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2025/02/07/u-s-copyright-office-issues-report-on-artificial-intelligence-and-copyrightability/)). Scenario states commercial rights for its platform models ([Scenario](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/commercial-use-licenses-of-scenario-platform-models)).
- Purchases: Apple's `verifyReceipt` is deprecated in favour of the App Store Server API and Notifications V2 ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/validating-iap-with-app-store-server-api/)).
